What is Google Ads?

Google Ads is an online advertising platform that allows businesses to display targeted advertisements to users searching for relevant products and services.

What are PPC advertising services?

PPC advertising services involve planning, creating, managing, and monitoring paid advertising campaigns to reach relevant audiences and generate website traffic or leads.
